The pomodoro timer that flies you to Saturn

FocusSpace turns the Pomodoro Technique into a journey through space. Every 25-minute focus fills a fuel cell; five cells take you to the next stop: the Moon, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn and beyond, with real distances.

FocusSpace on the App Store

How it works

  • Start a focus: 25 minutes, then a 5-minute break. Every fourth break is a long one.
  • Each finished focus fills a fuel cell and flies the rocket part of the real distance to the next planet.
  • Five pomodoros reach the next stop, with its facts: 15 stops from Earth to interstellar space.
  • The time left and the next stop stay on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. Pause, skip the break or add 5 minutes from there.

Questions

Is FocusSpace free?

Yes. The download, the Space Journey with all 15 stops, the classic 25/5 timer, three focus sounds, statistics and the Live Activity are free. FocusSpace Pro adds the plan, Apple Intelligence, every sound, themes and other lengths.

What is a pomodoro?

25 minutes of focus on one task, then a 5-minute break; every fourth break is a longer one. The Pomodoro Technique, step by step.

Do I need an account? Does it work offline?

No account, and it works offline. Your sessions, plans and check-ins stay on your device.

Which devices does it run on?

iPhone and iPad with iOS 18 or later, with layouts for iPad and the open iPhone Duo. Alarms that ring in silent mode need iOS 26 or later.

Does it use Apple Intelligence?

On supported devices FocusSpace Pro uses Apple Intelligence on the device for the day plan, the weekly recap and sentences like “Add: 2 hours thesis tomorrow at 10”. Nothing is sent to a server. Without Apple Intelligence the same features run on simple rules.

Which languages?

12: English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Japanese, Korean and Chinese (Simplified).
