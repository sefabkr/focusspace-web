Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

FocusSpace does not collect personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. What you do in the app stays on your device.

What stays on your device

  • Your focus sessions, the journey, statistics and settings.
  • Your plans, reminders, daily check-ins and mood entries.
  • Sounds you add from your files.

This data is stored in the app on your iPhone or iPad and is part of the device's own backup. It is never sent to the developer or to any server.

Apple Intelligence

On supported devices, FocusSpace Pro uses Apple's on-device model for the day plan, the weekly recap and understanding sentences you type. This runs on your device; nothing is sent to a server.

Notifications, alarms, widgets and Siri

Reminders, the end of a focus or a break, and alarms are scheduled on your device. The Live Activity, widgets, Control Center, Siri and Shortcuts read the app's data on your device. None of this leaves the device.

Purchases

FocusSpace Pro is sold by Apple through the App Store. The developer never sees your payment details. The app asks the App Store which plan you have, so it can open the Pro features.

Data we do not collect

  • Personal identifiers such as your name, email address or phone number.
  • Location.
  • Contacts, photos or other content on your device.
  • Usage analytics or advertising identifiers.

Third parties

FocusSpace contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code, and no third party receives data from it.

Children

FocusSpace is for a general audience. It does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Deleting your data

Use “Erase all data” in FocusSpace's settings, or delete the app.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and the date above will change with it.
