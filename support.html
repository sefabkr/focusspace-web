FocusSpace help

Answers to the questions people ask most. Anything else: write to sefabeker@gmail.com. The developer answers.

How does the journey work?

Every focus you finish fills one fuel cell. Five cells take the rocket to the next stop. There are 15 stops from Earth to interstellar space, at their real distances; after the last one a new lap begins.

Does a focus I stopped early count?

No. Only a focus that runs to the end fills a fuel cell. Pausing is fine: a paused focus counts when it ends.

How do I restore FocusSpace Pro on a new iPhone?

Open FocusSpace Pro from Settings or from any Pro feature and tap Restore purchases. Your purchase belongs to your Apple Account.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In the iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then FocusSpace. Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. If you started the free week, FocusSpace reminds you two days before it ends.

Notifications or alarms don't arrive.

Check Settings › Notifications › FocusSpace, and whether a Focus mode silences it. Alarms that ring in silent mode need iOS 26 or later and your permission for alarms.

Will I lose my journey if I delete the app?

FocusSpace keeps your data on your device only, and it is part of your iPhone's backup. Deleting the app deletes the data; restoring a backup brings it back.

Where is the New Year Focus Challenge?

From December 14 it is on the Journey screen. It runs from December 28 to January 27: finish 25 pomodoros and earn the 2027 mission patch.
